# Meditation and Relaxation App

A user-friendly Meditation and Relaxation App designed to reduce stress, improve mental well-being, and promote inner peace. It offers guided meditation, relaxing music, breathing exercises, and mindfulness activities to help users improve focus, enhance sleep quality, and maintain a healthy, peaceful lifestyle.

## Key Features
- Guided Meditation Sessions
- Relaxing Music
- Breathing Exercises
- Mindfulness Activities
- Stress Relief
- Sleep Improvement

**Goal:** To make mental wellness and relaxation accessible anytime, anywhere.
